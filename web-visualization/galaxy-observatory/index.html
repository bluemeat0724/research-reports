NGC 7741 Meridian

An SAB(rs)cd spiral observed well off the plane of its disk. Two logarithmic arms unwind from a compact warm bulge, dissolving at the rim into cobalt dust and sparse knots of young blue stars. The inner disk turns several times faster than the outer arms — a slow, patient winding, measured here in silence.
