The instruction to give your agent

Precise English

A few words make all the difference.

A compact vocabulary for unambiguous 3D instructions.

01

Rotate about an axis.

Yaw turns around Y. Pitch tilts around X. Roll turns around Z. These names use this lab’s Y-up, +Z-forward convention; always specify the axis.

02

Mirror across a plane.

A left–right reflection across the YZ plane negates X. An up–down reflection across the XZ plane negates Y. A mirror changes handedness; a rotation does not.

03

Keep the frame explicit.

World axes stay fixed. Local axes move with the phone. “Clockwise” depends on the viewpoint. A signed angle plus an axis is more precise than “flip it right.”
